# The student journey in the AHat portal

This guide follows one student from registration to settling in abroad. For each step it says what the **student** does, what the **staff member responsible** does, what the **portal** does by itself, and what moves the student on to the next step.

## Who looks after the student

| Person | Looks after | Assigned by | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Website enquiries; assigning everyone below; overseeing all students | – | – |
| **Counsellor** | 1. Career Counselling, 2. University & Country Selection, 3. Application Assistance. Stays the student's main contact throughout | Admin | Once the student's profile is complete |
| **Financial Aid Officer** | 4. Scholarship & Financial Guidance | Admin | Once Application Assistance is complete (the student has accepted an offer) |
| **Visa Officer** | 5. Visa Assistance, 6. Pre-Departure Support, 7. Post-Arrival Support | Admin | Once Scholarship & Financial Guidance is complete |

Once an officer is assigned, the counsellor can still **see** that officer's services but can't change them. Each officer can change only their own services. The admin can change anything.

## The journey at a glance

```
Register → Complete profile → Admin assigns counsellor
  → 1. Career counselling → 2. Shortlist → 3. Applications (offer accepted)
  → Admin assigns Financial Aid Officer → 4. Funding (plan covers the cost, proof of funds verified)
  → Admin assigns Visa Officer → 5. Visa (granted) → 6. Pre-departure (ready to travel) → 7. Post-arrival (settled)
```

The student's **progress tracker** shows the seven services. Each one is *Not started*, *In progress* or *Completed* (or *Not needed*), worked out from what has actually happened. Overall progress counts a completed service as 1 and one in progress as ½.

## Before registration: website enquiry (optional)

Someone who isn't ready to register can send an enquiry from the home page (name, email, mobile, destination, intake, course, best time to call, message).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visitor** | Sends the enquiry form. No account needed. |
| **Admin** | Finds it under **Leads** (filter "Admin (website enquiries)") and a "First contact with new enquiry" task on **Today**, due within 15 minutes in working hours (Mon–Sat 10:00–19:00), otherwise by 11:00 the next working day. Calls or messages the person, logs each attempt (channel and outcome), moves the enquiry through its stages, and asks them to register on the website. Can assign the enquiry to a counsellor to follow it up (this does **not** decide the student's counsellor later). |
| **Portal** | If the email or mobile already belongs to a student or an earlier enquiry, the enquiry is added to that record instead of creating a duplicate. When the person registers with the same email, the enquiry is marked **Registered** and linked to their new account. |

## Step A: Registration

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Clicks **Create your free account** on the website and enters full name, email, Indian mobile number and accepts the terms. Enters the 6-digit code emailed to them (valid 10 minutes; a new one can be requested after 30 seconds). |
| **Staff** | Nothing yet. |
| **Portal** | Creates the account and the student's record with a reference number (e.g. AH-2026-00093) and a starter document checklist: passport, degree certificate, transcripts, English test report, CV. **No counsellor is assigned.** The student signs in with a code sent by email each time; there is no password. |
| **Moves on when** | The account is created; the profile form opens straight away. |

## Step B: Complete the profile

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Fills in the **Complete your profile** form: date of birth, city; highest qualification, college or university, grade; English test and score (or "Not taken yet"); work experience (optional); course of interest, preferred destination, preferred intake, total budget. Can click **Later** to close it. |
| **Admin** | Sees students who haven't finished under **Assignments → Profile incomplete** and can click **Send reminder** (emails the student, at most once a day). |
| **Portal** | Until the profile is complete: the form opens at **every sign-in**; a banner "Complete your profile to start your journey" shows on every page; the tracker is replaced by a **Getting started** list; booking a session and messaging are blocked with a message asking the student to complete their profile. |
| **Moves on when** | Every required field is filled in. The student then joins the admin's **Waiting for a counsellor** list, the admin gets an "Assign a counsellor (profile complete)" task on **Today** (due within one working day), and the student sees "We're matching you with a counsellor who knows {country}". (The admin is not emailed at this point; they see it in the portal.) |

## Step C: Admin assigns a counsellor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Waits. Can update their profile from **My profile**. |
| **Admin** | Opens **Assignments → Waiting for a counsellor**. Each student shows their profile summary and the counsellors ranked by a match score out of 100: country experience (50), subject area (30) and spare capacity (20), with the reasons. Clicks **Assign** next to the chosen counsellor (an optional note is kept in the student's history). The suggestions come from each counsellor's matching profile (countries and years, subjects, universities, languages, capacity), which counsellors keep up to date themselves on their **My profile** page. Clicking a suggestion shows the reasons and the score breakdown. |
| **Portal** | Emails the student ("Meet your counsellor") and the counsellor ("New student assigned"). Closes the admin's task. The student now sees their counsellor's card and can book sessions and send messages. |
| **Moves on when** | A counsellor is assigned. The progress tracker appears. |

Later, if the student changes their course, destination or intake in their profile, the counsellor is emailed and the change is recorded in the student's history. The admin can **Reassign** a student to another counsellor (with a reason) from the student's record.

## 1. Career Counselling & Course Selection (counsellor)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Books a **Career counselling** session (button in the tracker, or **Sessions**): chooses a free time in the counsellor's calendar and online or at the office, and can add a note. Joins at the time with **Join meeting** (Google Meet) or comes to the office. |
| **Counsellor** | Sets their weekly availability, session length and time off in **Schedule**. Holds the session, then marks it **Completed** with notes (or **No-show**). Records the agreed **career plan** on the student's record: career direction, course, study level, destination countries and optional notes. Can update the plan later. |
| **Portal** | Sends both a calendar invitation with the meeting link, and the student a reminder the day before. The student sees the career plan in their tracker. |
| **Status** | *Not started* → *In progress* (a session has been booked) → **Completed** when the career plan is shared. |

## 2. University & Country Selection (counsellor)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Counsellor** | Builds the **shortlist** on the student's record: for each option, university, course, country, intake, tuition, living cost, application deadline, partner agency and why it suits the student. Clicks **Share** to send it to the student. Answers the student's questions on each option. |
| **Student** | Reviews the shortlist on **Applications**: for each option chooses **Apply** or **Remove** (with a reason), and can ask a question about any option. Clicks **Approve** once every option is decided (at least one to apply to). |
| **Portal** | Emails the student when the shortlist is shared and when the counsellor replies; emails the counsellor when the student asks a question or approves. On approval, creates a **draft application** for every option the student chose. If the counsellor adds an option after approval, the shortlist reopens for the student to decide on the new option. |
| **Status** | *In progress* while the shortlist is being prepared or reviewed → **Completed** when the student approves it. |

## 3. Application Assistance (counsellor)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Uploads the documents asked for on **Documents** (PDF, JPG or PNG, **50–200 KB** each). Re-uploads any document that was rejected. Watches application updates (new updates are marked "Updated"). When an offer arrives, clicks **Accept** or **Decline** (only one offer can be accepted). |
| **Counsellor** | Requests any further documents (e.g. Statement of Purpose, references). Checks each upload: **View**, then **Verify** or **Reject** with a reason the student sees. Prepares and submits each application, then updates its status as it changes: Submitted, Under review, More information requested, Interview, Conditional offer, Unconditional offer, Rejected, Withdrawn. If a status was chosen by mistake, uses **Correct it** (with a reason). Can book an **Application review** session. |
| **Portal** | Gives the counsellor a "Verify uploaded document" task for each upload (due within one working day). Emails the student every status change, and asks them to reply when an offer arrives. Reminds the counsellor 14, 7 and 2 days before a draft application's deadline (the admin is alerted at 2 days). Emails the counsellor when the student accepts or declines. |
| **Status** | *In progress* once an application is submitted → **Completed** when the student **accepts an offer**. |
| **Handover** | The admin gets an "Assign a Financial Aid Officer" task and an email, and the student joins **Assignments → Waiting for a Financial Aid Officer**. The student's tracker says "We're assigning your Financial Aid Officer". |

### Admin assigns a Financial Aid Officer
On **Assignments → Waiting for a Financial Aid Officer**, the admin clicks **Assign {officer}** (each button shows that officer's current number of students). The student, the officer and the counsellor are emailed. The officer appears on the student's dashboard with their own chat and booking times.

## 4. Scholarship & Financial Guidance (Financial Aid Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Financial Aid Officer** | Sets the **estimated total cost of study** (₹ lakh). Suggests **scholarships** (name, amount, deadline) and records the result (awarded / not awarded). Requests the **proof of funds** documents the student must show (e.g. bank statement held for 28 days for the UK, blocked account confirmation for Germany, loan sanction letter, sponsor letter) and verifies or rejects each upload. Holds **Scholarship & funding** sessions and answers questions in their own chat. |
| **Student** | Builds their **funding plan** on **Funding & visa**: adds each source (family savings, education loan, scholarship, sponsor, other) with its amount until it covers the estimated cost. Marks a scholarship as **Applied** when they apply. Uploads the proof of funds documents. Can book a **Scholarship & funding** session with the officer and message them. |
| **Portal** | Gives the officer the "Verify uploaded document" tasks for proof of funds. Reminds the officer (task) and the student (email) 14, 7 and 2 days before a scholarship deadline that hasn't been applied for. The counsellor can see this service but not change it. |
| **Status** | *In progress* once the funding plan, a scholarship or a proof-of-funds upload exists → **Completed** when **the funding plan covers the estimated cost and every proof of funds document is verified** (scholarships are optional). The officer can mark it **Not needed** (with a reason) for a student who needs no help. |
| **Handover** | The admin gets an "Assign a Visa Officer" task and an email, and the student joins **Assignments → Waiting for a Visa Officer**. If the service was marked *Not needed* and no Financial Aid Officer was assigned, the student goes straight to this list. |

### Admin assigns a Visa Officer
Same as above, from **Assignments → Waiting for a Visa Officer**. The student, the officer and the counsellor are emailed.

## 5. Visa Assistance (Visa Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visa Officer** | Requests the **visa documents** (e.g. CAS copy, TB test certificate) and verifies or rejects each upload. Records each stage on the **Visa** tab as it happens, with an optional note: 1. CAS or admission letter received, 2. Visa documents checked, 3. Visa fee and health surcharge paid, 4. Visa application submitted (with the visa reference, e.g. GWF0123456), 5. Biometrics appointment done. Then records the decision: **Visa granted**, or **Visa refused** with the reason (the student sees it) and starts again from "Visa documents checked" if they reapply. Holds **Visa preparation** sessions (e.g. mock credibility interview). |
| **Student** | Uploads the visa documents, pays fees and attends biometrics as guided, books **Visa preparation** sessions and messages the officer. |
| **Portal** | Emails the student at every stage. Gives the officer a "Check visa decision status" task 15 days after submission (closed when the decision is recorded). |
| **Status** | *In progress* from the first stage → **Completed** when the visa is **granted**. |

## 6. Pre-Departure Support (Visa Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Ticks off the six tasks on **Travel** as they're done: accommodation booked; flight booked; travel and health insurance arranged; forex card or bank account ready; pre-departure briefing attended; travel documents packed (passport, visa, offer, CAS or admission letter). Books the **Pre-departure briefing** session. |
| **Visa Officer** | Runs the pre-departure briefing, follows progress on the **Pre-departure** tab and, when all six tasks are ticked, clicks **Confirm ready to travel** (this locks the checklist). |
| **Status** | Starts when the visa is granted → **Completed** when the officer confirms the student is ready to travel. |

## 7. Post-Arrival Support (Visa Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Clicks **Confirm arrival** on **Travel** once they've arrived safely. Books **Post-arrival check-in** sessions and messages the officer about settling in. |
| **Visa Officer** | Gets an email when the student arrives and a "1-week check-in after arrival" task. Holds check-ins during the first months. Records the **final check-in** with a note, either when completing the last Post-arrival check-in session (tick "final check-in") or with **Record final check-in** on the **Post-arrival** tab. |
| **Status** | *In progress* from the arrival confirmation → **Completed** when the final check-in is recorded. The student's stage becomes **Settled** and the journey is complete. |

## Throughout the journey

**Sessions**
- Each kind of session is with the person who looks after it: career counselling and application review with the counsellor; scholarship & funding with the Financial Aid Officer; visa preparation, pre-departure briefing and post-arrival check-in with the Visa Officer. Topics whose officer isn't assigned yet can't be booked.
- Students book free times only, with the notice each staff member sets, and can have up to **3 upcoming sessions**. Online sessions use AHat's Google Meet room.
- Students can cancel before a session starts. Staff can book on a student's behalf, reschedule (with a reason the student sees), cancel (with a reason), and mark sessions completed (with notes) or as a no-show.
- Everyone gets a calendar invitation for each booking, change and cancellation; students get a reminder the day before.

**Messages**
- The student has a **separate chat** with each person looking after them (counsellor, Financial Aid Officer, Visa Officer), each with its own unread count. Each staff member sees only their own chat; the admin can open all of them.
- An email is sent for a new message at most once an hour per chat.

**Documents**
- Staff request documents for their own services: application documents (counsellor), proof of funds (Financial Aid Officer), visa documents (Visa Officer). Each upload creates a "verify" task for that person.
- Files are stored privately; links to view them work for a few minutes and only for the student and staff allowed to see them.

**Notifications and tasks**
- The student's **Notifications** card lists what's waiting on them (messages, application updates, documents to upload or re-upload, offers to reply to, the shortlist to review, the funding plan, travel tasks, upcoming sessions).
- Staff see their tasks on **Today**. A task that becomes overdue alerts the administrators by email once.
- Non-urgent emails to students wait until 08:00 if they would be sent between 21:00 and 08:00.

**Changing a service's status by hand**
- For real exceptions, the person responsible for a service can use **Change status** (e.g. *Not needed* for a self-funded student), with a reason. It's shown to the student and kept in the history; it can be set back to automatic.

**History**
- Every step, status change, upload, verification, assignment and note is kept in the student's history with who did it and when. Staff can add private notes.

**Signing in and staying signed in**
- Students sign in with a code emailed to them; they stay signed in for up to 30 days (7 days without activity).
- Staff sign in with a password and an authenticator app; they're signed out after 30 minutes without activity or 12 hours after signing in.
