# The student journey in the AHat portal

This guide follows one student from registration to settling in abroad. For each stage it says what the **student** does, what the **staff member responsible** does, what the **portal** does by itself, and what moves the status on.

## Who looks after the student

| Person | Looks after | How they are assigned | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Website enquiries, and overseeing all students. Steps in when a student can't be assigned automatically | – | – |
| **Counsellor** | 1. Career Counselling & University Selection, 2. Application Assistance. Stays the student's main contact throughout | Automatically, the best match with room | When the student's profile is complete |
| **Financial Aid Officer** | 3. Scholarship & Financial Guidance | Automatically, the same one for every new student | When the student's profile is complete |
| **Visa Officer** | 4. Visa Assistance, 5. Pre-Departure Support, 6. Post-Arrival Support | Automatically, the same one for every new student | When the student's profile is complete |

**Funding runs alongside the first stages**, so the Financial Aid Officer can start as soon as they're assigned, without waiting for the applications. The Visa Officer is assigned at the same time, but their stages only start once an offer is accepted and funding is complete (see the status rules below).

Once an officer is assigned, the counsellor can still **see** that officer's stages but can't change them. Each officer can change only their own stages. The admin can change anything.

If no counsellor has room, or the single officer is unavailable, nothing is assigned to anyone else: the student appears on the admin's **Assignments** page and the admin assigns someone by hand.

## The journey at a glance

```
Register → Verify email → Complete profile → Counsellor and both officers assigned automatically
  → These run at the same time:
       1. Career counselling and university shortlist (plan recorded, shortlist shared)
          → 2. Applications (shortlist approved, offer accepted)
       3. Funding (plan covers the cost, proof of funds verified)
  → Once an offer is accepted and funding is complete:
       4. Visa (granted) → 5. Pre-departure (ready to travel) → 6. Post-arrival (settled)
```

The student's **progress tracker** shows these six stages.

## How each stage's status updates

Nobody types a status in. The portal works out each stage's status from what has actually happened in the student's record, every time the page loads. The student and staff always see the same result.

There are four statuses: **Not started**, **In progress**, **Completed**, and **Not needed** (only ever set by a person).

| Stage | Not started | Becomes In progress when | Becomes Completed when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Career Counselling & University Selection** | Until a counsellor is assigned | A **counsellor is assigned**. The student then sees "Next: Book a career counselling session" with a **Book a session** button | **Both** are done: the career plan is recorded **and** the shortlist is shared with the student |
| **2. Application Assistance** | Until the counsellor shares the shortlist | **Any** of these: the shortlist is **shared with the student** (the student then sees "Next: Review and approve your shortlist" with a **Review shortlist** button); the student approves the shortlist; an application exists | The student **accepts an offer** |
| **3. Scholarship & Financial Guidance** | Until the student has both a counsellor and a Financial Aid Officer. It doesn't wait for the other stages | **Either** of these: a counsellor and a Financial Aid Officer are assigned, which is the same moment as stage 1 for new students; or funding work has begun (a funding source, a scholarship or a proof of funds upload) | The funding plan **covers the estimated cost and** every proof of funds document is verified. Scholarships are optional. There must be at least one proof of funds document |
| **4. Visa Assistance** | Until an offer is accepted and funding is complete, even though the officer is already assigned | **Either** of these: an offer is accepted, funding is complete and a Visa Officer is assigned (the student sees "Waiting for your CAS or admission letter to arrive"); or the first visa step is recorded | The visa is **granted** |
| **5. Pre-Departure Support** | Until the visa is granted | The visa is granted | The Visa Officer clicks **Confirm ready to travel**, which needs all six tasks ticked |
| **6. Post-Arrival Support** | Until the student confirms arrival | The student clicks **Confirm arrival** | The **final check-in** is recorded |

### Things worth knowing

- **Approving the shortlist belongs to stage 2.** Stage 1 completes when the shortlist is shared, not when it's approved. Until the student approves, stage 2 stays **Not started**, even though its first step shows as in progress under **View details**.
- **Stage 1 doesn't go backwards.** If the counsellor revises the shortlist, or a session is cancelled, stage 1 keeps its status.
- **A refused visa stays In progress**, and the student is told their officer will talk them through the next steps.
- **Some stages can go back.** Their status always reflects the record as it stands now:
  - Stage 2 returns to In progress if an accepted application is withdrawn.
  - Stage 3 returns to In progress if a verified proof of funds document is removed or a new one is requested, or if the estimated cost is raised above the funding plan.
- **Requesting a document at the Funding stage counts as proof of funds**, so stage 3 can't complete until that document is verified.
- **Students who registered before automatic assignment** may have no officer. When they reach that stage they see that the officer is being assigned, and the admin assigns one.

### Changing a status by hand

For real exceptions, the person responsible for a stage can click **Change status** and choose Not started, In progress, Completed or Not needed, with a reason. An example is *Not needed* for a self-funded student.

- It replaces the automatic status until it's set back to automatic.
- The student sees it, and it's kept in the history. Staff also see what the automatic status would have been.
- Marking a stage Completed or Not needed lets the next stage start. Marking stage 1 this way also starts stage 2. Marking funding this way counts as funding complete for the visa stage, which still needs an accepted offer.

### Overall progress

The percentage on the dashboard counts a completed stage as 1 and an in-progress stage as ½, out of all stages. Stages marked Not needed are left out.

### The Next line and buttons

Under a stage, the tracker can show a **Next** line and a button when something is waiting, such as **Book a session**, **Review shortlist**, **Reply to offer**, **Upload documents**, **Add funding plan**, **Open travel checklist** or **Confirm arrival**. **View details** lists that stage's steps as Done, In progress or Coming up.

### Stages staff see for each officer

On an officer's **Today** page, students who haven't reached that officer's stage are listed under **Upcoming** and don't count towards **Needs you**. They move into the officer's working list when the stage starts. For the Financial Aid Officer that's as soon as the student has a counsellor, and for the Visa Officer it's once an offer is accepted and funding is complete.

### The record stage on staff screens

Separately from the six statuses, each student's record shows one overall label, which only moves forward:

| Event | Label |
|---|---|
| Student registers | Registered client |
| Shortlist shared | Documents and shortlist |
| First application submitted | Applications submitted |
| Offer accepted | Offer accepted |
| Visa application submitted | Visa filed |
| Visa granted | Visa granted |
| Arrival confirmed | Arrived |
| Final check-in recorded | Settled |

## Before registration: website enquiry (optional)

Someone who isn't ready to register can send an enquiry from the home page (name, email, mobile, destination, intake, course, best time to call, message).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visitor** | Sends the enquiry form. No account needed. |
| **Admin** | Finds it under **Leads** and as a "First contact with new enquiry" task, due within 15 minutes in working hours (Mon–Sat 10:00–19:00), otherwise by 11:00 the next working day. Calls or messages the person, logs each attempt, moves the enquiry through its stages, and asks them to register. Can assign the enquiry to a counsellor to follow it up (this does **not** decide the student's counsellor later). |
| **Portal** | If the email or mobile already belongs to a student or an earlier enquiry, the enquiry is added to that record instead of creating a duplicate. When the person registers with the same email, the enquiry is marked **Registered** and linked to their new account. |

## Step A: Registration

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Clicks **Create your free account** and enters full name, email, Indian mobile number and a password, and accepts the terms. Enters the 6-digit code emailed to them to verify their email (valid 10 minutes; a new one can be requested after 30 seconds). Someone who came from a referral link sees a short note saying so. |
| **Staff** | Nothing yet. |
| **Portal** | Creates the account and the student's record with a reference number (e.g. AH-2026-00093) and a starter document checklist: passport, degree certificate, academic transcripts and updated CV. No counsellor or officer is assigned yet. If the person was referred, the referral is linked. |
| **Moves on when** | The email is verified and the student is signed in. |

## Step B: Complete the profile

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Fills in **My profile**: date of birth, mobile, gender (optional), permanent address; courses of interest (up to 3), preferred destination and intake; English test and score (or "Not taken yet"); work experience (optional). |
| **Highest qualification** | Chosen from a dropdown, and the fields follow the choice. For **12th / HSC** the student gives their board, school or junior college and year, with stream and result optional. For any other qualification they give the college or university and year, with specialisation and result optional. **Other** lets them type the qualification's name. |
| **Other qualifications** | Optional. **Add qualification** adds their 12th or diploma and their 10th, each of which can be removed again. |
| **Admin** | Sees students who haven't finished under **Assignments → Profile incomplete** and can click **Send reminder** (at most once a day). |
| **Portal** | Until the profile is complete, the Overview shows a **Complete your profile** button, a banner shows on other pages, and booking a session is blocked. |
| **Moves on when** | Every required field is filled in. |

## Step C: Automatic assignment

| | |
|---|---|
| **Portal** | As soon as the profile is complete, it assigns the single Financial Aid Officer and Visa Officer, and the best-matched counsellor who has room. Counsellors are ranked out of 100 on country experience (50), subject area (30) and spare capacity (20), using the matching profile each counsellor keeps up to date on **My profile**. |
| **Student** | Sees their team on the dashboard, gets a "Meet your counsellor" email, and can book sessions and send messages. |
| **Counsellor** | Gets an email and a notification. The student appears in **New students** on **Today** until the record is opened. |
| **Officers** | Get a notification, not an email, for each new student. |
| **Admin** | Does nothing unless no counsellor has room. Then the student joins **Assignments → Waiting for a counsellor**, the admin gets a task, an email and a notification, and assigns someone by hand from the same ranked list. |

The admin can **Reassign** a student to another counsellor, or change an officer, with a reason. If the student later changes their course, destination or intake, the counsellor is emailed and the change is recorded.

## 1. Career Counselling & University Selection (counsellor)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Books a **Career counselling** session: chooses a free time, online or at the office, and can add a note. Joins with **Join meeting** (Google Meet) or comes to the office. Sees the career plan and the shortlist once shared. |
| **Counsellor** | Sets their availability in **Schedule**. Holds the session and marks it **Completed** with notes (or **No-show**). Records the **career plan**: direction, course, study level, destinations and notes. Builds the **shortlist**: for each option the university, course, country, intake, duration, tuition, living cost, application deadline and why it suits the student. Clicks **Share**. |
| **Portal** | Sends calendar invitations with the meeting link, and the student a reminder the day before. Emails the student when the shortlist is shared. |
| **Status** | See the status rules above. **Completed** when the career plan is recorded and the shortlist is shared. |

## 2. Application Assistance (counsellor)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Reviews the shortlist on **Applications**: for each option chooses **Apply** or **Remove** (with a reason), and can ask questions. Clicks **Approve** once every option is decided, with at least one to apply to. Uploads the documents asked for on **Documents** (PDF, JPG or PNG, 1–300 KB each), or invites a parent or sponsor to upload them. Re-uploads anything rejected. When an offer arrives, clicks **Accept** or **Decline** (only one offer can be accepted). |
| **Counsellor** | Requests further documents. Checks each upload: **View**, then **Verify** or **Reject** with a reason (a document must be opened before it can be verified or rejected). Prepares and submits each application, then updates its status: Submitted, Under review, More information requested, Interview, Conditional offer, Unconditional offer, Rejected, Withdrawn. Uses **Correct it** (with a reason) for a mistake. Can book an **Application review** session. |
| **Portal** | On approval, creates a **draft application** for each chosen option. If the counsellor adds an option afterwards, the shortlist reopens for the student to decide on it. Gives the checker a "Verify uploaded document" task for each upload (one working day). Emails the student every status change. Reminds the counsellor 14, 7 and 2 days before a draft application's deadline, and alerts the admin at 2 days. |
| **Status** | See the status rules above. **Completed** when the student accepts an offer. |

## 3. Scholarship & Financial Guidance (Financial Aid Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Financial Aid Officer** | Sets the **estimated total cost of study** (₹ lakh). Suggests **scholarships** and records the result. Requests **proof of funds** documents and verifies or rejects each upload. Holds **Scholarship & funding** sessions and answers questions in their own chat. |
| **Counsellor** | Can see this stage but not change it, with one exception: they can **edit or delete** a suggested scholarship. The student is emailed, and the officer is notified. |
| **Student** | Builds their **funding plan** on **Funding & visa**: adds each source (family savings, education loan, scholarship, sponsor, other) with its amount until it covers the cost. Marks a scholarship as **Applied**. Uploads proof of funds, or a parent or sponsor does. A **Book a session** button shows on this stage until a Scholarship & funding session is booked by the student or staff. |
| **Portal** | Shows **Funding & visa** in the student's navigation once this stage starts, alongside stage 1. Gives the officer the verify tasks. Reminds the officer and the student 14, 7 and 2 days before a scholarship deadline that hasn't been applied for. |
| **Status** | See the status rules above. **Completed** when the plan covers the cost and all proof of funds is verified. The officer can mark it **Not needed** with a reason. |

## 4. Visa Assistance (Visa Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Visa Officer** | Requests visa documents and verifies or rejects each upload. Records each step with an optional note: 1. CAS or admission letter received, 2. Visa documents checked, 3. Visa fee and health surcharge paid, 4. Visa application submitted (with the reference), 5. Biometrics appointment done. Then records **Visa granted**, or **Visa refused** with the reason (the student sees it) and restarts from "Visa documents checked" if they reapply. Holds **Visa preparation** sessions. |
| **Student** | Uploads visa documents, pays fees and attends biometrics as guided, and books sessions and messages the officer. A **Book a session** button shows on this stage until a Visa preparation session is booked by the student or staff. |
| **Portal** | Emails the student at every step. Gives the officer a "Check visa decision status" task 15 days after submission. |
| **Status** | See the status rules above. **Completed** when the visa is granted. |

## 5. Pre-Departure Support (Visa Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Ticks off six tasks on **Travel**: accommodation booked; flight booked; travel and health insurance arranged; forex card or bank account ready; pre-departure briefing attended; travel documents packed. Books the **Pre-departure briefing** session. |
| **Visa Officer** | Runs the briefing, follows progress and, when all six tasks are ticked, clicks **Confirm ready to travel**, which locks the checklist. |
| **Status** | See the status rules above. **Completed** when the officer confirms. |

## 6. Post-Arrival Support (Visa Officer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Student** | Clicks **Confirm arrival** on **Travel** once they've arrived safely. Books **Post-arrival check-in** sessions and messages the officer. |
| **Visa Officer** | Gets an email and a "1-week check-in after arrival" task. Holds check-ins during the first months. Records the **final check-in** with a note, when completing the last check-in session or with **Record final check-in**. |
| **Status** | See the status rules above. **Completed** when the final check-in is recorded, and the student's record becomes **Settled**. |

## Throughout the journey

**Sessions**
- Each kind of session is with the person who looks after it: career counselling and application review with the counsellor; scholarship & funding with the Financial Aid Officer; visa preparation, pre-departure briefing and post-arrival check-in with the Visa Officer.
- Students book free times only, and can have up to **3 upcoming sessions**. Online sessions use AHat's Google Meet room.
- Students can cancel before a session starts. Staff can book for a student, reschedule or cancel with a reason, and mark sessions completed or as a no-show.
- Everyone gets a calendar invitation for each booking, change and cancellation. Students get a reminder the day before.

**Messages**
- The student has a **separate chat** with each person looking after them, each with its own unread count. Each staff member sees only their own chat, and the admin can open all of them.
- An email is sent for a new message at most once an hour per chat.

**Documents**
- Staff request documents with a dropdown under every stage of the student's journey, or choose **Other** to type a name. Documents already on the student's checklist aren't offered again.
- Requests go to the part of the checklist for that stage: applications, proof of funds or visa. Each upload creates a "verify" task for the person responsible.
- The student sees an **Upload** button on the stage where the document was requested, which opens **Documents**. It goes once everything requested there is uploaded, and comes back if a document is rejected.
- Staff must open a document before they can verify or reject it. The reject window also has a **View document** button.
- Files are stored privately. Links to view them work for a few minutes and only for the student and staff allowed to see them.

**Parents and sponsors**
- From **Documents**, a student can invite a parent, guardian or sponsor. That person gets a private link by email and needs no account.
- The link shows only the documents still needed, and only lets them upload. They can't open files or see anything else about the student.
- A link lasts 30 days and the student can cancel it at any time. A student can have up to 5 people invited at once.
- Uploads go through the same review, and show who uploaded them. The student is emailed.

**Refer a Friend/Family**
- Students use **Refer a friend**, and counsellors use **Referrals**. Each has a personal link, and can also invite someone by email. Admins see everyone's referrals.
- Each referred person shows as **Invited**, **Registered**, **Profile complete**, **Applied** or **Offer accepted**, with totals at the top. The status updates by itself from the person's record.
- Referrers see progress only, not the person's email or record.

**Explore Universities**
- Students search courses by destination, program level, study area, intake and year, and sort the list by Featured, QS Ranking or Tuition Fees. Each course shows when its figures were checked, and a link to the university's page where one is given.
- The **Eligibility** panel compares their qualification, undergraduate and 12th scores, backlogs and English test with each course, and says why they don't meet it. Only what they enter is checked.
- The list comes from the spreadsheet an admin uploads under **Courses**. Each upload replaces the whole list, and nothing is saved if any row has a problem. **Load demo list** fills it with a starter set of real courses, taken from each university's website on the date shown.

**Notifications and tasks**
- The student's **Notifications** card has two parts. **Updates** lists everything staff have done on the student's journey, such as a document verified, a visa step or a session moved. Each update goes once it's opened or marked as read. **To do** lists what's waiting on the student, and each item goes once it's done. New messages show under To do. Private staff notes, staff tasks and draft shortlists aren't shown.
- Staff see a **Notifications** card on **Today** (admins on **Overview**) with their own updates: students assigned, sessions, messages, documents, shortlist and offer decisions. Each one goes once it's opened or marked as read, and some clear by themselves once dealt with.
- Staff tasks are on **Today**. A task that becomes overdue alerts the administrators by email once.
- Admin alert emails go to info@ahat.in.
- Non-urgent emails to students wait until 08:00 if they would be sent between 21:00 and 08:00.

**History**
- Every step, status change, upload, verification, assignment and note is kept in the student's history with who did it and when. Staff can add private notes.

**Signing in and staying signed in**
- Students log in with their email and password, and can reset a forgotten password with an emailed code. They stay signed in for up to 30 days (7 days without activity).
- Staff log in with a password and an authenticator app. They're signed out after 30 minutes without activity or 12 hours after signing in.
